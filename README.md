
# BioSecBench-Respvirus-Countermeasures

**Benchmarking the analytical reasoning of AI agents for respiratory virus countermeasures**

RespVirus-Counter tests whether AI agents can analyze biological data, laboratory results, and patient records to understand how respiratory viruses cause illness, how immunity protects people, and whether vaccines and treatments remain effective as viruses change. These capabilities support research on preventing infections and reducing severe disease. Scores show the share of attempts that reach the correct answer, counting refusals and errors as failures. A higher score means the model is more accurate at these tasks.

In this repo, there are 3 public tasks that DO NOT APPEAR in the benchmark, but mimic benchmark like questions.
